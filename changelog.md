# Changelog

## 0.4

- Credits cap at 10,000 plus 500 per population. Losing population throws away anything above the new cap.
- Factories and helipads take 4 population, shipyards 8, and airports 12.
- Factory waypoint keeps sending new units to the nearest open tile once the point itself is taken.
- Airport and jet, helipad and gunship, and shipyard and cruiser, each with its own movement.
- Cruisers take move orders on water. Shipyards can only be placed on water.
- Airports are a 2×4 landing strip. Jets take off and land on it, circle an ordered tile, and can be sent home. Gunships climb off the pad and can be sent back.
- Jets carry five rockets. A rocket accelerates, and it splashes harder than a shell. An empty jet returns to the airport that launched it to reload.
- Cruisers turn a little faster, reach a higher top speed, and fire out to 15. Jets see out to 21.
- Scouts are 30% slower.
- Jets fire only after they have left the ground.

## 0.3

- Cities and trees can be destroyed. 
- Armies require populations to sustain and build.
- Improved unit steering & pathing in narow paths
- Reduce artilery range
- Increased tank armours

## 0.2

- Conquer cities and hold them to earn credits. Leave units in a city and it grows.
- Improved fog of war.
- Construction spends credits while the work is underway, not when you queue it.
- Turn the camera with Q and E.
- Forests are denser.

## 0.1

- Island skirmish with selection, move, and attack orders.
- Pan, rotate, and zoom the camera.
